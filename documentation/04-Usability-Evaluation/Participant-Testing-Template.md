## Facilitator introduction

“We are evaluating the RecipeHub interface, not your ability. You may stop at any time. Please do not enter real passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. The payment scenario will use a test environment or be discussed without completing a transaction.”

## Tasks

Ask the participant to think aloud if comfortable. Do not guide them unless they become blocked; record any assistance provided.

1. From the home page, find recipes you might want to cook.
2. Open one recipe and locate its ingredients, instructions, and reviews.
3. Find where you would sign in or create an account.
4. As a signed-in test account, create a recipe draft or complete a test recipe form without uploading personal content.
5. Like a recipe and add it to favorites, then find the saved favorite.
6. Submit a test review, then locate how to edit it.
7. Explain what you expect to happen when selecting a premium recipe purchase action. Do not enter live payment information.
8. If using a designated administrator test account, locate the tools for reviewing reports. Do not change real user or recipe records.

## Optional post-task questions

1. Which action or page was easiest to understand? What made it clear?
2. Where did you hesitate or need to try more than once?
3. Were messages after submitting forms clear and useful?
4. Could you tell which information was public and which actions required sign-in?
5. What would you change first?

## Reporting results

Report the actual number of participants and their relevant context only when sessions have occurred. Separate direct observations from facilitator interpretation. Do not generalize beyond the participants and devices tested. Do not publish identifying information or unconsented recordings.
